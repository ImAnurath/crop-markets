Giresun in-shell price
The 2014 frost took the price from $2.92 to $7.59 in 14 months; the 2025 short crop, from $3.69 to $7.56 in 9 months.
Shaded: the 2005, 2014-15 and 2025 supply shocks. The TL view uses a log scale.
Where the price could be in 6 months
If past 6-month moves repeat, eight times in ten the April 2027 price lands between $2.95 and $5.83.
