Hazelnut price forecasting
Every forecasting method we tried against naive (the price stays where it is): the development scoreboard, calm and shock months, the holdout, ranges, crop estimates and TL forecasts.
Crops / Hazelnut / Analyses / Forecasting
Can anything beat "the price stays where it is"?
Data as of October 2026 · refreshed weekly (some sources from December 2025)
What we found
Nothing we tried beat naive, the forecast that next month's price is this month's, at 1, 3 or 6 months ahead in the development period. On the holdout (scored once, never tuned on) no method beat it at every horizon. In supply shocks the errors grew 2.0 to 2.7 times, naive's included. What can honestly be offered is a range: the spread of past moves gave an 8-in-10 range that held 84% of the time 3 months ahead.

Crop estimates: national instead of Giresun, and a month later

Ranges, USD
How wide the honest range is
The 8-in-10 range, 3 months ahead
3 months ahead, the 8-in-10 range held 84% of the time (197 of 234 months); 7 of the 37 misses fell in a shock period.
Naive range (past moves): the price 3 months earlier times the 10th and 90th percentiles of all earlier 3-month moves. Development period, USD per kg; marked months fell outside the range.
Range methods compared
An honest 8-in-10 range holds about 80% of the time. Naive range (past moves) held 82% to 84%, and no other method scored better at any horizon.

TL prices
Forecasting in TL
Last USD price × the lira's depreciation trend
In the crisis months its error was 7% to 39% below naive's; in calm months it was within 2% of naive's.
MAPE divided by TL naive's, development period (36-month trend). Lira crises: May to December 2018 and September 2021 to December 2023. This is the lira's own depreciation trend, in the 2018-24 crisis years, suggestive (6 variants tried); it is no hazelnut-market signal.
TL forecasts against TL naive
In percent terms nothing beat naive at 1 month; at 3 and 6 months the depreciation-trend forecasts had 3% to 7% lower error, suggestive (6 variants tried).
