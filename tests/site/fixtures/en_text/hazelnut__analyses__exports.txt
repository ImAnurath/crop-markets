Hazelnut exports
Where Turkey’s shelled hazelnut exports go, how the destinations have shifted since 2000, and what a kilo of kernels earns against the exchange price.
Crops / Hazelnut / Analyses / Exports
Where the hazelnuts go
Data as of October 2026 · refreshed weekly (some sources from December 2025)
What we found
Italy and Germany took 51% of shelled export volume in 2025. Italy's share has gone from 13% in 2000 to 29%, while Germany's went from 40% to 22%. A kilo of shelled nuts has sold abroad for about 2.1 times the exchange's in-shell price, roughly the two kilos of nuts in shell it takes to make one.
Destinations over time
Italy's share of shelled export volume went from 13% in 2000 to 29% in 2025; Germany's from 40% to 22%.
Share of shelled export volume (net weight) by calendar year, for the 3 largest destinations of 2025; complete years only, the rest went elsewhere. Source: UN Comtrade.
What a kilo of kernels earns
A kilo of shelled exports sold for a median 2.05 times the exchange's in-shell price (8 in 10 months between 1.85 and 2.36 times).
Shelled exports: FOB value divided by net weight, all destinations (UN Comtrade). Giresun in-shell: the exchange's monthly average. Shelling roughly halves the weight, which accounts for most of the gap; in-shell exports are too small to compare.
